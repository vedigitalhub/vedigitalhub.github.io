How it works

Saving stops meaning forgetting

Everyone saves dozens of posts a week and never finds them again. In Savvi, every save becomes something you use.

1

Share it to Savvi

In Instagram, TikTok or any app, tap Share and pick Savvi. You don't even leave the app you're in.

2

AI organizes it

It understands the content, writes a title and a summary and files it under the right category: place, recipe, trip, product, movie, book…

3

It becomes a plan

Places show up on the map, recipes send ingredients to the shopping list and everything can be found by searching the way you talk.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does Savvi download Instagram and TikTok videos?

No. Savvi keeps the link and reads the post's public information (caption and cover). When the key details are only in the audio, you can send a screen recording, which is deleted right after the analysis.

Do I need Premium?

No. On the free plan you can save as much as you like and the AI organizes 5 items a month. Map, recipes, shopping list and search work on both plans.

Which languages does it support?

English, Portuguese and Spanish. The AI writes titles and summaries in the language you choose, even when the post is in another language.

How do I cancel my subscription?

In your App Store or Google Play subscriptions. Premium stays active until the end of the period already paid. There's a shortcut in the app's Settings.

How do I delete my account?

In Settings › Delete account. See the account deletion page for details.
